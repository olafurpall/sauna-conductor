      Your sessions are private to you and kept in the cloud, so they're on every device you sign in on. To work on one with others, open it and press Invite or Share link. Privacy

      Spotify access

      While the Spotify app is in development mode, Spotify lets in only the people on its user list, 5 at most. People Spotify turned away ask here. Add them at developer.spotify.com → Thora sauna conductor → User Management with their Spotify email, then mark them as added: they see it and can connect.

        Callout recordings

        Invite people to record callouts: short lines like “Gerum þetta!” or “Síðasta lagið!” in their own voice. They get a private link to a page where they record each line on their phone or computer, with no account. When they press Send, their callouts can be added to any session. Callouts page

          Spotify

          Press Connect Spotify and log in with your own Spotify account (Premium). There's nothing else to set up: Sauna Conductor already has its Spotify app. While Sauna Conductor is in beta, Spotify only lets in the people on its guest list: if Spotify turns you away, you'll be asked for the email you use for Spotify so you can be added. Your Spotify login is saved in your account, so your other devices connect by themselves.

          This browser is set to a different Spotify app than the shared one, which is why connecting may fail.
          Advanced: use a different Spotify app (not needed)

          Narration (ElevenLabs)

          Everyone records narration through the app's server, with your ElevenLabs account. The key stays on the server (Supabase → Edge Functions → Secrets, or the app_secrets table); no browser ever sees it. Each person can record up to 25,000 characters a day; you have no limit. Your own and cloned voices are only for you.

          ElevenLabs

          Narration is recorded with your own ElevenLabs account. Create an API key at elevenlabs.io → Developers → API keys with access to Text to Speech, Voices (read and write), Models and User (read). Setting a credit limit on the key is a good idea. The key is stored in this browser and, when you're signed in, in your workspace so the people you share with can record too.

          Talk button

          While a session runs, 🎙 Talk in the middle of the ring lets you speak to the group: the music dips, and if the narrator is speaking it fades out (you finish the message yourself). Press Resume to bring the music back up. Key: T.
