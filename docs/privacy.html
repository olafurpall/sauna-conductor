Privacy

Sauna Conductor, sauna.roadtalk.io. Last updated 5 October 2026.

Sauna Conductor is a small private tool for running sauna sessions: it plays music from Spotify between recorded narration. It is used by invitation, and it is not sold or advertised.

Signing in

When you sign in with Google or with an emailed link, the app receives your name and email address. They are used only to sign you in and to show who belongs to a shared workspace. The app does not ask Google for anything else.

What is stored

  • Sessions you create: playlists chosen, timing, narration text, voice settings and the narration audio, including messages you upload or record with your microphone. The microphone is used only while you record a message, after you choose Record it now.
  • A history of the sessions you ran.
  • Callouts: short clips recorded by people the site's administrator invited. They record on a page that needs no account, and agree there that the recordings can be played in sessions; their name is shown with them. They can ask for them to be removed at any time.
  • Your own Spotify login (a renewable access token), so your other computers don't need to connect again. Only you can read it.

This is kept in the browser you use and in a database hosted by Supabase in Europe. Access rules in the database let only you, and the people you share a session with, see your sessions, recordings and history.

Spotify and ElevenLabs

Music plays through your own Spotify account, and narration is recorded with ElevenLabs through the app's server. Their own privacy terms apply to what you do on their services.

Sharing

Nothing is shared with anyone you haven't shared it with, and nothing is used for advertising or analytics.

Removing your data

You can delete sessions in the app at any time, sign out, and disconnect Spotify in Settings. To have your account and everything stored for it removed, ask the person who invited you, or the site owner, and it will be deleted.
